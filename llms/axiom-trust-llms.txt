## About

Axiom Trust Company was founded in 2025 by David Meister and is headquartered in
Las Vegas, Nevada. It operates as a regulated corporate trustee combined with an
internal software platform for trust administration.

Positioning: Axiom provides regulated infrastructure and operational precision
for modern fiduciaries and private families without competing for the client
relationship. It does not replace the advisor or the attorney.

Service models:
- Independent Trustee — executes an advisor's investment strategy under the
  advisor's brand, handling the administrative burden.
- Directed Trustee — the client retains discretion, investment authority, and
  family governance structures; Axiom executes the mandate and works with the
  client's preferred custodian.
- Delegated administration — bill pay, notices, distributions, approvals, and
  principal & income accounting, with an audit-ready record of decisions.

Segments served: investment advisors, attorneys and trustees, and private
families.

Complex mandates accepted: closely held entities, private investments,
contested situations, and special needs trusts.

Funding: $11.8M in pre-seed and seed funding led by Lightspeed Venture Partners,
with Wischoff Ventures, Runa Capital, SNR, Primetime Partners, and Mercury
founder Immad Akhund participating.
